Design Philosophy

I notice what others walk past: the small friction, the unspoken pattern, the system behind the screen. I hold my work to evidence over applause. Depth is my default.

My design inspiration comes from the world around me and what I observe, the patterns I notice in people. I am curious about art, culture, and people.

Design Process

There is no one size fits all process. Every problem carries its own context, its own constraints, and its own definition of done.

01

Understanding the Why

I start by asking why the work needs to exist and who it is really for. Once the context is clear, everything downstream gets easier to decide.

02

Finding the Ground Truths

I talk to the people closest to the problem before I open a design tool. Assumptions get tested, not inherited.

03

Designing in the Open

Wireframes, prototypes, and reviews happen in public, not behind a curtain. The work gets sharper every time someone pushes back on it.

04

Building It, Not Just Handing It Off

I stay close through implementation. A design that never ships, or ships wrong, has not actually solved anything.

05

Working Faster With AI

AI speeds up research and lets me try more directions before committing to one. It also makes prototyping fast enough to do live with stakeholders and users, helping them shape the product the way they envision it.
