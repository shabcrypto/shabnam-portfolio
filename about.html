The longer story

A seeker, from the Malabar Coast.

Shabnam grew up on the Malabar Coast of Kerala, the land of lords and looms: Theyyam ritual performance and handloom weaving. Where she is from, craft and devotion share the same word, and that idea has shaped how she works ever since.

She does the research herself, sits with users and stakeholders until the ground truths surface, then designs and builds end to end. Six years in design, three plus in high-stakes health InsurTech, shipping B2B SaaS used by 21,000+ hospitals across India, the GCC, and Kenya. At IHX, colleagues have a shorthand for the standard: “do something Shabnam to it.”

She calls herself a collector of memories, conversations, and experiences. She defines herself as a seeker: she finds purpose in whatever she does, and believes learning is a continuous process and a career is a journey.

Ambivert. Objective. Inquisitive. Allergic to generic.
